## U-009 — source categories (status: resolved from snapshot)

- `mods.json` has 16 784 rows; 322 are `domain: "desecrated"` prefixes/suffixes (147 / 175).
- Equipment bases have `domain: "item"`, regular jewels `misc`, Waystones (class `Map`) `area`.
  Regular source = `mod.domain === base.domain`; exclusive = `domain === "desecrated"`.
- Named Lich rows carry ordinary base tags first (e.g. `armour:1, ring:1, …, default:0`) and the
  Lich tag (`ulaman_mod:1`) afterwards. They are therefore naturally spawnable on matching bases,
  consistent with SoT §7.3's correction.
- Otherworldly: 36 desecrated rows with a positive `breach_desecration` entry and zero-weight
  `amulet`/`ring`/`belt` entries, exactly as SoT §7.6 describes.
- Desecrated rows gated only by special tags (`watcher_abyss_suffix`, `kulemak_abyss_prefix`,
  `kulemak_abyss_special_prefix`) never match ordinary base tags; they belong to special unique
  systems (U-005) and fall out of ordinary pools by spawn-weight semantics.
- Desecrated rows with `map` tags are Waystone (Preserved Vertebrae) mods.
